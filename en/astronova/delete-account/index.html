Astronova account and data deletion

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account, and the data linked to it, in Astronova: Space Chase, developed by Yelkovan. You only have an account if you signed in to the game with Apple or Google; if you never signed in, no account data has reached us.

1. Delete from inside the game

  1. Open Astronova and make sure you are signed in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If the game says "Sign in again, then retry the delete", you need a recent sign-in for security reasons: sign out, sign in again with the same account and repeat the deletion.

2. Delete by email

If you can't open the game or have uninstalled it, email info@yelkovan.ai with the subject "Astronova account deletion". Include the email address of the Apple or Google account you signed in with and, if you have one, your pilot name on the leaderboard. We may ask for more information to confirm the request is yours. We complete your request within 30 days at the latest and let you know.

3. What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication): user ID (UID), email address and name.
  • Your cloud save: pilot name, coin and emerald balances, inventory, scores, stats, campaign progress, milestones, challenge streak and purchase transaction IDs.
  • Your leaderboard rows: your row on the All-time board and your rows on the daily boards. Deleting from inside the game removes the daily rows for the recent days your device has on record; if an older daily row remains, just write to us and we will delete it too. Requests by email remove all daily rows.

4. What is kept

  • Progress on your device: your coins, characters and records stay on your device and you can keep playing offline. Your pilot name and that day's Daily Run attempt are reset. All data on the device is removed when you uninstall the game.
  • Purchase records: Apple or Google keep records of your purchases under their own legal obligations. The purchase record at RevenueCat is tied to an anonymous ID, not to your sign-in account; mention it in your email if you want it deleted.
  • Ad data: data processed by Google AdMob is kept under Google's own policies. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Without deleting your account

  • Sign out: Settings → Account → Sign out. Your cloud save and leaderboard rows are not deleted.
  • Change your pilot name: the new name also shows on the leaderboards.
