Astronova

An endless, one-tap vertical climb through space. Cast your hook, speed up, dodge and keep rising.

GameFreeiOS · Android7 languages
App Store Google Play

One tap

Cast the hook, swing on the rope and launch. It takes seconds to learn and far longer to master.

Campaign and Daily Run

A ten-level campaign that ends in a fight with the Captain. A new Daily Run every day, with one scored attempt.

Play offline

No account and no internet needed. If you like, sign in with Apple or Google to back up your progress and race on the leaderboards.

Good to know

  • The game is free. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases (emerald packs).
  • App Store age rating: 9+.
  • Languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi.
