Jackall privacy addendum

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This addendum is part of the Yelkovan Privacy Policy and covers what is specific to the Jackall app. The data controller, your rights and how to contact us are in the main policy.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you make stays on your phone; we have no server.
  • The app goes online only to download public exchange rates (European Central Bank) and, if you use the translator, language packs. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Camera, microphone, photos and location are used only inside the tool you open and are processed on your phone.
  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

1. Publisher

Jackall is developed and published by Yelkovan (Yiğit Özdemir). The app's technical ID is com.ay.caki.

2. Where your data is

Your Library, recent work, My info, flows, settings and copies of photos or files you import are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. On iPhone, the app's shared container (App Group) is also used by widgets, Control Center, the Share extension and Shortcuts; on Android, widgets and shortcuts read from the app's own storage. We cannot access this data.

If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is on, the operating system may include the app's data in it. That backup is in your account and follows your settings.

3. Internet

For the currency converter, the European Central Bank's public rates file (ecb.europa.eu) is downloaded, at most once an hour. Nothing about you is sent with it; like any website, the ECB can see the IP address the request comes from. Without a connection the app uses the last rates it has.

The translator works on your phone. On iPhone, iOS downloads the language packs it needs (Apple's on-device translation, iOS 18 and later). On Android, Google's on-device translation library (ML Kit) downloads language models from Google. The text you translate is never sent. Apart from this, Jackall makes no network requests. Links you open and things you share are handled by the app you choose.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: QR and barcode scanning, document and photo scanning, passport photo, magnifier and similar tools.
  • Microphone: sound level meter, tuner, voice recorder and speech-to-text. Speech is recognised on the device.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick (gallery cleanup reads your library on your phone to find similar photos); saving results to Photos.
  • Location (while using): prayer times, qibla and sun/moon times for where you are. It is not stored or sent; you can pick a city instead.
  • Motion and sensors: compass, spirit level and altitude.
  • Notifications: timers, focus sessions and prayer time alerts, scheduled on your phone.

Permissions are asked only when you first use a tool that needs them. Images, audio and location are processed on your phone; unless you save them, they are not kept, and they are never uploaded.

5. Widgets, Shortcuts and Share

If you add widgets or Live Activities, summary information (for example the next prayer time, a countdown or a rate) is written where they can read it on your phone. Anyone who sees your screen can see it. Shortcuts and the Share menu pass what you choose to the app on your phone only.

6. Purchase

Jackall is a one-time purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

7. What we don't use

  • No ads, advertising ID or tracking; on iPhone you are never asked for tracking permission.
  • No analytics or crash reporting software (SDK).
  • No account, sign-in or server.
  • We don't sell or share your data; it never reaches us.

8. Deletion

You can delete items from the Library and My info at any time. Deleting the app from your phone deletes all of its data (except your phone's own backup).

9. Contact

Questions: info@yelkovan.ai. When you write to us, we use your email address only to reply. If this addendum changes, the new version is published on this page.
