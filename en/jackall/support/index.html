Jackall support

If your question isn't answered below, write to info@yelkovan.ai. Including your phone model, OS version and the app version (at the bottom of Settings) helps us help you faster.

How do I find a tool?

The Tools tab lists every tool by group; the search field at the top finds a tool by name or by what you want to do ("pdf", "currency", "passport photo"). Tap the star in a tool to add it to your favourites.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every tool works without internet. Exchange rates refresh when you're online and the last rates are used offline. The translator needs its language packs downloaded once.

The translator says a language isn't available

On iPhone, on-device translation needs iOS 18 or later; the tool offers to download the language pack the first time. On Android, the language model is downloaded once by Google's on-device translation library. Some language pairs may not be supported on your device.

A tool asks for a permission

Camera, microphone, photos, location and notifications are asked for only when a tool needs them. If you declined, you can turn it on later: on iPhone, Settings › Jackall; on Android, Settings › Apps › Jackall › Permissions.

How do I add a widget?

iPhone: touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen › Edit › Add Widget › search for "Jackall". You can also add Jackall controls to Control Center and use its actions in Shortcuts and Siri.

Android: touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen › Widgets › "Jackall". Touch and hold the app icon for shortcuts, and add the QR tile from Quick Settings.

Prayer times look off

Check the city or location in the prayer times tool and the calculation method in its settings; the method follows your country by default. Results are for information; check your local authority for exact times.

Purchase, refunds and family

Jackall is a one-time purchase; there are no subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases. You can download it again on your other devices with the same account. Refunds follow Apple's rules on the App Store and Google's on Google Play; you request them there. On iPhone you can share it with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I change the language?

Settings › Language. Jackall is available in 44 languages. Your country, home currency and city are in Settings too.

I found a bug or have an idea for a tool

Write to info@yelkovan.ai. A screenshot helps.
