Map & More terms addendum

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This addendum is part of the Yelkovan Terms of Use and sets out the rules specific to the Map & More trip planner.

1. Free plan and Pro

Map & More is free to download and start using. On the free plan:

  • 1 trip in total (past trips count, sample trips don't; delete a trip to start a new one) and 30 things in My bags;
  • some suggested places and route stops are locked;
  • outfits for the first 2 days of a trip and the first 2 local phrases are open;
  • ads are shown (see 2).

Everything else is open on the free plan too.

Pro removes the ads and the free plan's limit on trips and things, and unlocks every suggested place, the full route and Pro picks for each day, an outfit for every day, every local phrase, the year in review, splitting costs with travel companions, adding to your calendar and pasting bookings. What Pro includes is shown in the app's Pro panel before you buy.

On both the free plan and Pro there is a daily limit on plans and searches to prevent abuse. Trips and things you added before going Pro are not deleted. If Pro ends, the free plan's limits and locks apply again; your existing trips and things are not deleted, only adding new ones is limited.

2. Ads

On the free plan the app shows a banner marked "Ad" in My trips and an occasional full-screen ad after you create a trip. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are non-personalised. The products and services in ads belong to the advertisers; we don't recommend them and are not responsible for them. Pro has no ads. What data is processed for ads is in the privacy addendum.

3. Buying Pro

Options. Pro is sold in two ways: an auto-renewing yearly subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices vary by country and are shown in the Pro panel, in the store's currency, before you buy.

Payment. Apple takes the payment on the App Store and Google on Google Play; purchases are also subject to the store's own terms. Payment is charged when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends if you started with one.

Auto-renewal. The yearly subscription renews automatically every year unless you cancel, and the yearly price is charged to your store account. On the App Store, it renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the last 24 hours of the period; on Google Play, it is charged at the start of the new period. If the price changes, the store tells you in advance and asks for your consent where required.

Free trial. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial of the yearly subscription. If you don't cancel before the trial ends (on the App Store, at least 24 hours before), the subscription starts and the yearly price is charged. The store decides who is eligible; if you have used this subscription or an introductory offer before, the trial may not be offered. A yearly subscription bought with the welcome offer has no trial.

Welcome offer. When you first open the Pro panel, a 24-hour welcome offer starts: half the regular price (rounded to the store's nearest price point) for the first year of the yearly subscription, or once on the lifetime purchase. The countdown starts only once; when it ends, the offer is not shown again. A yearly subscription bought with the welcome offer renews at the regular yearly price after the first year. The store decides who is eligible; on the App Store, if you are not eligible, the yearly offer is not shown.

Lifetime. Lifetime Pro is a one-time payment, not a subscription: it does not renew and you are not charged again. You can use Pro with the Map & More account you bought it with for as long as Map & More is offered.

Cancelling. You can cancel the subscription at any time; Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Map & More Pro → Cancel Subscription (Apple's guide).
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Map & More → Cancel subscription (Google's guide).
  • In the app, Profile → Manage subscription also opens the store's subscription page.

Deleting the app or your Map & More account does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds. The store takes the payment, so the store also handles refund requests; we can't issue refunds because we don't receive the payment. App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: Google Play refund policies. If a purchase is refunded, Pro turns off. Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected.

Restoring. Pro is tied to the Map & More account you bought it with and turns on on every phone where you sign in with that account. If it doesn't, tap Profile → Restore purchases.

Changes. We may change Pro's price and content, or the free plan's limits, from time to time; we announce changes in the app and on this page. The price of an existing subscription changes only under the store's rules (with advance notice and, where required, your consent).

4. Check suggestions before you go

Day plans, place descriptions and stay suggestions are generated automatically with AI; they are not written or individually reviewed by a person. Places are checked against OpenStreetMap, but:

  • opening hours, prices, addresses and whether a place is still open may have changed;
  • walking and flight times, distances, weather (climate averages), plugs, money, tipping and tap water information are estimates;
  • suggestions may be wrong, incomplete or not right for you.

Before you go, check with the place's own website or official sources. For visas, entry requirements, health and safety, rely only on official sources. You are responsible for decisions you make based on the suggestions.

Map & More does not make bookings and does not sell tickets or hotel stays. Suggested places and hotels are not ads; nobody pays us to be suggested. Ads (see 2) are never mixed into your plan or the suggestions.

5. Account

You need an account to see your plan. Use a valid email address and keep your account secure. Don't use the app for illegal purposes, and don't try to overload the server, get around the limits or interfere with how the app works. If we notice abuse, we may suspend or close the account.

6. Content you enter

Your trips, notes and photos are yours. We store them only to show them on your phone and to back them up to your account and bring them back. Enter your travel companions' names and your travel documents at your own discretion, and don't enter other people's sensitive information without their permission. You don't have to enter passport or ID numbers.

7. Limits of the backup

We work to keep the cloud backup reliable, but we can't guarantee it will always be available. Keep the originals of important documents such as tickets, passports and bookings separately.

We may change the list of cities, the features, the daily limits and the suggestion service from time to time. For changes to Pro and the free plan, see 3.
