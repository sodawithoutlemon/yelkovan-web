Map & More terms addendum

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This addendum is part of the Yelkovan Terms of Use and sets out the rules specific to the Map & More trip planner.

1. It's free

Map & More is free. The app has no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. To prevent abuse there is a daily limit on plans and searches. If we ever add a paid feature, we will announce it in the app and on this page in advance.

2. Check suggestions before you go

Day plans, place descriptions and stay suggestions are generated automatically with AI; they are not written or individually reviewed by a person. Places are checked against OpenStreetMap, but:

  • opening hours, prices, addresses and whether a place is still open may have changed;
  • walking and flight times, distances, weather (climate averages), plugs, money, tipping and tap water information are estimates;
  • suggestions may be wrong, incomplete or not right for you.

Before you go, check with the place's own website or official sources. For visas, entry requirements, health and safety, rely only on official sources. You are responsible for decisions you make based on the suggestions.

Map & More does not make bookings and does not sell tickets or hotel stays. Suggested places and hotels are not ads; nobody pays us to be suggested.

3. Account

You need an account to see your plan. Use a valid email address and keep your account secure. Don't use the app for illegal purposes, and don't try to overload the server, get around the limits or interfere with how the app works. If we notice abuse, we may suspend or close the account.

4. Content you enter

Your trips, notes and photos are yours. We store them only to show them on your phone and to back them up to your account and bring them back. Enter your travel companions' names and your travel documents at your own discretion, and don't enter other people's sensitive information without their permission. You don't have to enter passport or ID numbers.

5. Limits of the backup

We work to keep the cloud backup reliable, but we can't guarantee it will always be available. Keep the originals of important documents such as tickets, passports and bookings separately.

6. Map and data sources

Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), OpenMapTiles and OpenFreeMap; world map Natural Earth; place photos from Wikimedia Commons, each under its own licence (author and licence under the photo); climate averages Open-Meteo and ERA5 / Copernicus (CC BY 4.0). The full list is in the app under Profile → Licences.

7. Changes to the service

We may change the list of cities, the features, the daily limits and the suggestion service from time to time.

8. App Store users

If you downloaded the app from the App Store, Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (standard EULA) applies. The Yelkovan Terms of Use and this addendum supplement the EULA.

9. Google Play users

If you downloaded the app from Google Play, the Google Play Terms of Service also apply.
