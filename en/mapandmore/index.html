Map & More

Plan your trip day by day on a map and pack from your own closet. Free to start; go Pro for no ads, every place and an outfit for every day.

TravelFree + ProiOS and Android45 languages
App Store Google Play

Day-by-day plan

Real places, day by day: museums, viewpoints, places to eat. Every day on a map, with walking times between stops.

Tear off your ticket

Pick your home city and your destination on the map, and Map & More prints you a boarding pass. Flight time, time difference and that month's weather are right on the ticket.

Packing and outfits

A packing list for the weather and the length of your trip, and an outfit for each day from your own closet. Plugs, money and tipping before you go.

Good to know

  • Day plans are prepared by AI. Places are checked against OpenStreetMap, but check opening hours, prices and addresses before you go.
  • The app is free to download and start using. The free plan has 1 trip and 30 things in My bags, and shows ads. Pro (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) removes the ads and limits and unlocks every place, outfit and tool.
  • To see your plan you sign up once (Apple, Google or email). The account is there for the cloud backup, so your trips are safe if you change phones.
  • The app has no analytics or tracking and does not access your location. Ads on the free plan are non-personalised.
  • Day plans for 150 cities; your home city can be anywhere in the world. iPhone, iPad and Android. 45 languages.
  • Map & More is developed by Yelkovan (Yiğit Özdemir).
