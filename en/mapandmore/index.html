Map & More

Plan your trip day by day on a map, pick the neighbourhood to stay in and pack from your own closet. Completely free: no ads, no subscription.

TravelFreeiOS and Android45 languages

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Day-by-day plan

Real places, day by day: museums, viewpoints, places to eat. Every day on a map, with walking times between stops.

Where to stay

Compare neighbourhoods on the map: close to the metro, quiet, lively at night. Your own hotel is on the map too.

Packing and outfits

A packing list for the weather and the length of your trip, and an outfit for each day from your own closet. Plugs, money and tipping before you go.

Good to know

  • Day plans and stay suggestions are prepared by AI. Places are checked against OpenStreetMap, but check opening hours, prices and addresses before you go.
  • The app is completely free: no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.
  • To see your plan you sign up once (Apple, Google or email). The account is there for the cloud backup, so your trips are safe if you change phones.
  • The app has no analytics, ads or tracking, and does not access your location.
  • Day plans for 150 cities; your home city can be anywhere in the world. iPhone, iPad and Android. 45 languages.
  • Map & More is developed by Yelkovan (Yiğit Özdemir).
