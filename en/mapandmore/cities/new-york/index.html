What to pack for New York

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 3 °C / 38 °F, lows -5 °C / 24 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 14 °C / 57 °F, lows 4 °C / 39 °F, about 12 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 27 °C / 81 °F, lows 17 °C / 63 °F, about 13 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 18 °C / 64 °F, lows 8 °C / 46 °F, about 9 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 120 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC-5 (UTC-4 in summer).
