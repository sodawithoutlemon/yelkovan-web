What to pack for Barcelona

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 13 °C / 55 °F, lows 5 °C / 42 °F, about 6 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 18 °C / 64 °F, lows 10 °C / 50 °F, about 7 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 26 °C / 80 °F, lows 19 °C / 67 °F, about 6 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 20 °C / 68 °F, lows 14 °C / 57 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 230 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+1 (UTC+2 in summer).
