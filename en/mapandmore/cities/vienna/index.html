Weather in Vienna by month

What to pack for Vienna

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 4 °C / 39 °F, lows -2 °C / 28 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 15 °C / 59 °F, lows 5 °C / 42 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 25 °C / 77 °F, lows 15 °C / 60 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 15 °C / 58 °F, lows 7 °C / 45 °F, about 9 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 230 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+1 (UTC+2 in summer).
