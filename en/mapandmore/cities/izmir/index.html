What to pack for Izmir

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 13 °C / 55 °F, lows 4 °C / 40 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 21 °C / 70 °F, lows 10 °C / 50 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 34 °C / 93 °F, lows 21 °C / 70 °F, about 2 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 24 °C / 75 °F, lows 14 °C / 57 °F, about 6 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
