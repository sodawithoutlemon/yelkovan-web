Weather in London by month

What to pack for London

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 8 °C / 46 °F, lows 2 °C / 36 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 14 °C / 56 °F, lows 5 °C / 42 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 21 °C / 70 °F, lows 13 °C / 55 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 15 °C / 58 °F, lows 8 °C / 46 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
