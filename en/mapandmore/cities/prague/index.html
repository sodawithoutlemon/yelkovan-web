What to pack for Prague

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 4 °C / 39 °F, lows -2 °C / 28 °F, about 9 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 14 °C / 58 °F, lows 4 °C / 40 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 24 °C / 75 °F, lows 14 °C / 57 °F, about 12 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 14 °C / 57 °F, lows 6 °C / 43 °F, about 9 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 230 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+1 (UTC+2 in summer).
