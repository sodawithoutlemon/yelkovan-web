What to pack for Dubai

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 24 °C / 76 °F, lows 19 °C / 65 °F, about 2 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 31 °C / 88 °F, lows 23 °C / 74 °F, about 2 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 39 °C / 102 °F, lows 31 °C / 87 °F, about 0 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 34 °C / 93 °F, lows 27 °C / 81 °F, about 1 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 220 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+4.

A few local words

  • Al-ḥisāb min faḍlak (الحساب من فضلك) — the bill, please
  • Bikam hādhā? (بكم هذا؟) — how much is it?
  • Ayna al-ḥammām? (أين الحمام؟) — where is the toilet?
