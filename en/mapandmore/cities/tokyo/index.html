What to pack for Tokyo

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 10 °C / 51 °F, lows 2 °C / 35 °F, about 7 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 18 °C / 65 °F, lows 9 °C / 48 °F, about 12 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 29 °C / 84 °F, lows 21 °C / 70 °F, about 14 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 22 °C / 72 °F, lows 15 °C / 58 °F, about 13 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 100 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the left.
  • Time zone: UTC+9.

A few local words

  • Onegaishimasu (お願いします) — please
  • Okaikei onegaishimasu (お会計お願いします) — the bill, please
  • Ikura desu ka? (いくらですか？) — how much is it?
  • Toire wa doko desu ka? (トイレはどこですか？) — where is the toilet?
