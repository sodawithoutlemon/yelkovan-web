What to pack for Antalya

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 14 °C / 56 °F, lows 6 °C / 43 °F, about 12 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 20 °C / 69 °F, lows 10 °C / 51 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 33 °C / 92 °F, lows 22 °C / 71 °F, about 1 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 25 °C / 77 °F, lows 15 °C / 59 °F, about 6 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
