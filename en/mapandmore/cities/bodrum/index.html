What to pack for Bodrum

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 14 °C / 57 °F, lows 10 °C / 50 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 19 °C / 65 °F, lows 14 °C / 57 °F, about 6 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 28 °C / 82 °F, lows 23 °C / 73 °F, about 1 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 23 °C / 73 °F, lows 18 °C / 64 °F, about 5 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
