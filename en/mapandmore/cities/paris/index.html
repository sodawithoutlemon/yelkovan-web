What to pack for Paris

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 8 °C / 46 °F, lows 2 °C / 35 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 15 °C / 59 °F, lows 6 °C / 43 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 23 °C / 74 °F, lows 14 °C / 57 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 16 °C / 60 °F, lows 8 °C / 47 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
