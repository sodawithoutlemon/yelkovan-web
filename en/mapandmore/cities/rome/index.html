What to pack for Rome

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 12 °C / 54 °F, lows 4 °C / 39 °F, about 9 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 19 °C / 65 °F, lows 9 °C / 48 °F, about 10 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 29 °C / 85 °F, lows 19 °C / 65 °F, about 5 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 21 °C / 70 °F, lows 13 °C / 55 °F, about 11 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 230 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+1 (UTC+2 in summer).
