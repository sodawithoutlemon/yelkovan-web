What to pack for Athens

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 13 °C / 55 °F, lows 6 °C / 43 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 20 °C / 68 °F, lows 11 °C / 52 °F, about 6 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 32 °C / 89 °F, lows 21 °C / 70 °F, about 2 rainy days a month. Pack: light, breathable clothes, a sun hat and sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 23 °C / 73 °F, lows 15 °C / 59 °F, about 4 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 230 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+2 (UTC+3 in summer).

A few local words

  • Parakaló (Παρακαλώ) — please
  • Ton logariasmó, parakaló (Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ) — the bill, please
  • Póso kánei? (Πόσο κάνει;) — how much is it?
  • Poú eínai i toualéta? (Πού είναι η τουαλέτα;) — where is the toilet?
