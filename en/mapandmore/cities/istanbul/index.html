What to pack for Istanbul

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 9 °C / 48 °F, lows 4 °C / 40 °F, about 12 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a warm hat or scarf for early mornings and evenings, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 16 °C / 60 °F, lows 9 °C / 48 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 27 °C / 80 °F, lows 20 °C / 68 °F, about 4 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 19 °C / 67 °F, lows 14 °C / 57 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
