What to pack for Lisbon

Packing notes from those averages:

  • Winter (Dec–Feb): highs about 14 °C / 57 °F, lows 9 °C / 49 °F, about 9 rainy days a month. Pack: warm layers and a proper jacket, a waterproof jacket or a compact umbrella, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Spring (Mar–May): highs about 18 °C / 65 °F, lows 12 °C / 54 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug): highs about 25 °C / 78 °F, lows 18 °C / 64 °F, about 1 rainy days a month. Pack: t-shirts and light trousers or skirts, plus one light layer for the evening, comfortable walking shoes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): highs about 21 °C / 70 °F, lows 15 °C / 59 °F, about 8 rainy days a month. Pack: layers: a long-sleeve top, a light jacket or sweater, comfortable walking shoes.

Good to know before you go

  • Voltage: 230 V. Check your plug type before you go.
  • Traffic drives on the right.
  • Time zone: UTC+0 (UTC+1 in summer).
