Telveli

Take a photo of your coffee cup and Telveli writes your reading with AI. Tarot, palm reading, dream meanings and your daily horoscope, all in one app.

EntertainmentFreeAndroid4 languages
Google Play

Coffee cup reading from a photo

Turn your cup over, let it cool and take a photo. You get a notification when your reading is ready.

Tarot and palm reading

Pick three cards from the deck, or send photos of your left and right palm.

Dreams, horoscope, birth chart

Describe a dream, read your daily horoscope, or get a birth chart reading from your birth date, time and city.

Good to know

  • Readings are written by artificial intelligence, not by a human fortune teller.
  • Telveli is for entertainment only. Readings are not predictions of real events and are not advice on health, money, legal or personal decisions.
  • The app is free. Readings use tokens; you get free tokens every day and can earn more by watching rewarded ads. The optional Telveli Premium subscription (weekly, monthly, yearly) unlocks the premium readings and removes ads.
  • The free version shows ads.
  • Languages: English, Turkish, Arabic and Hindi.
  • On Google Play, Telveli is published under the developer name "A&Y Creations"; the app is developed by Yelkovan (Yiğit Özdemir).
